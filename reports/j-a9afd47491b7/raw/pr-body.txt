## Remaining limitations

This is an honest partial eager-path contribution, not evidence of complete feature integration. CUDA-graph replay, sliding-window attention, encoder-decoder cross attention, speculative decode, and dequant-workspace KV cache configurations still use ordinary decode. The prepared node is AMD ROCm and the pinned interpreter has no `flashinfer`, so real NVIDIA kernel execution, wrapper/cache-layout compatibility, and end-to-end serving performance remain unverified. The qualified tiny Llama fixture cannot exercise FlashInfer on this architecture and was not used as unrelated proof. No native source changed, so no native rebuild was applicable.
