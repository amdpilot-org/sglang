At that commit, both SGLang connector entry points expose a classic split-pool
contract:

  LMCacheLayerwiseConnector(..., k_pool: List[Tensor], v_pool: List[Tensor], ...)
  LMCacheMPConnector(..., k_pool: list[Tensor], v_pool: list[Tensor], ...)

The in-process adapter sets its registered caches to k_pool for MLA or
k_pool + v_pool for MHA. The multiprocess adapter validates non-empty,
equal-length K and V layer lists, flattens them, and registers the layout hint
"k_v". Neither API can describe the DeepSeek-V4 SWA, C4, C4 indexer,
C128, and state regions or their different index sources/page geometry.

Required external work: LMCache must add a grouped-pool registration and
transfer contract (the SGLang patch probes for a `pool_group` constructor
parameter) and implement it for both layerwise and multiprocess paths. Until
then SGLang raises NotImplementedError after successfully resolving the real
physical pool group, rather than fabricating k_pool/v_pool.
